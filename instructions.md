Based on a deep study of the provided "Islamic Azad University, Bandar Abbas Branch - Master's Thesis / Doctoral Dissertation Writing Guide," here are all the essential points you need to consider for writing your master's thesis.

This guide, dated 2014, outlines the mandatory structure, formatting, and submission requirements. The principles are highly detailed and must be followed precisely.

### 1. Overall Structure and Page Order

The thesis must be printed double-sided on A4 paper. The page order is fixed and divided into two sections: unnumbered preliminary pages and numbered main pages.

**Preliminary Pages (Numbered with Abjad Letters: ا, ب, ج, etc.)**

1. **Blank Page**
2. **Cover Page** (in Persian)
3. **"Bismillah" Page** (Keep it simple, no decorative frames)
4. **Jury Approval Form** (Signed after defense and corrections)
5. **Originality of Thesis Undertaking**
6. **Research Ethics Charter**
7. **Dedication** (Maximum one page)
8. **Acknowledgments** (One page)
9. **Table of Contents** (Includes lists of tables, figures, maps, etc., but does *not* list the preliminary pages themselves like dedication or acknowledgment)
10. **List of Tables**
11. **List of Charts, Photos, and Maps**
12. **List of Abbreviations, Signs, and Symbols**

**Main Pages (Numbered with Digits: 1, 2, 3, etc.)**

1. **Persian Abstract** (Starts at page 1. Maximum 250 words in one page. Keywords are mandatory.)
2. **Main Text Chapters** (Continues numbering from the abstract)
   * Chapter 1: Introduction (Statement of the problem, importance, objectives, hypotheses)
   * Chapter 2: Literature Review & Research Background
   * Chapter 3: Research Methodology
   * Chapter 4: Data Analysis and Findings
   * Chapter 5: Conclusion and Suggestions
3. **References** (Continues numbering)
   * Persian Sources
   * Latin/English Sources
4. **Appendices** (Continues numbering, labeled with letters (A, B) if multiple)
5. **English Abstract** (Continues numbering)
6. **English Title Page**
7. **English Back Cover** (Gold-embossed)
8. **Blank Page**

- **Important Note for Table of Contents:** The "Table of Contents" must include the headings for chapters, sections, and sub-sections with their page numbers. Sub-headings should be indented. It should *not* list the preliminary pages (like Dedication, Acknowledgment, Abstract).

### 2. Content and Writing Style Detailed Rules

#### Chapters & Text

- **Typing Software:** Microsoft Word.
- **Font:**
  - Main text: `B Lotus` (or similar), size 14, thin.
  - Titles: `B Lotus`, size 14, **bold**.
  - Footnotes: `B Lotus`, size 12.
  - Table, Figure, Chart titles: `B Lotus`, size 11, thin.
  - Equations: `Times New Roman`, size 12. Indices/powers: `Lotus`, size 9.
- **Line Spacing:** Single (1.0).
- **Page Margins (Persian Text):**
  - Right: 3 cm
  - Left: 2 cm
  - Top & Bottom: 3 cm
  - Page number position: 1.5 cm from the bottom edge, perfectly centered.
- **Page Margins (English/Latin Text):** The margins are mirrored.
  - Right: 2 cm
  - Left: 3 cm (or 2.5 cm and 3.5 cm as another note specifies, but the chapter text rule states 2 and 3 cm). The key is that the wider margin is on the **left** for English text.

#### Figures, Tables, and Formulas

- They must be clear, independent, and understandable without reading the main text.
- **Numbering:** Uses a two-part system separated by a hyphen: `Chapter Number` - `Sequential Number` (e.g., Figure 2-5 is the 5th figure in Chapter 2).
- **Table Specifics:**
  - Title and number are placed **above** the table (in the center or top right corner).
  - Uses horizontal/vertical lines or just spacing to separate data.
  - Footnotes for the table go directly below it, with independent numbering from the text footnotes.
- **Chart/Figure/Photo Specifics:**
  - The title and number are placed **below** the chart/figure.
  - Avoid large pages. If unavoidable, fold them so they don't stick out.
- **Formula Specifics:** Numbered like (2-1). Formulas in appendices are numbered like (A-2).

#### Referencing System (Harvard Style)

The guide specifically recommends the **APA/Harvard style** (author-date) and mandates consistency.

- **In-text Citation:**
  - Reference as (Author, Year).
  - For three authors: List all three every time (Bond, Norrish & Burton, 1991).
  - For four or more authors: Use the first author's last name followed by "et al." (McCaskey et al., 1981).
  - Direct quotes require the page number (Reinking & Bell, 1991, p.368).
  - Personal communications (letters, emails, lectures) are cited in the text only (e.g., R. Pugliese, personal communication, March 23, 1990) and are **not** included in the reference list.
- **Reference List (فهرست منابع):**
  - Must list **every** source cited in the text. Do not include sources you only read but didn't cite.
  - Separate lists for Persian and Latin/English sources.
  - In a Persian thesis: Persian sources are listed first, then Latin sources. Both lists are ordered alphabetically by the first author's last name.
  - Do not include titles like "Dr.," "Prof.," "Engineer" before names unless they are an inseparable part of the name.

### 3. Critical Procedural and Formatting Points

- **Section Numbering:** The preliminary section pages are numbered with the Persian Abjad alphabet (الف, ب, ج, د, etc.). The main body pages (from the abstract onwards) are numbered with standard digits (1, 2, 3...). This numbering continues uninterrupted through the English pages at the end.
- **Golden Embossing:** The title and the author's name are embossed in gold on the spine of the cover and on the English back cover.
- **Date Formats:** The Persian cover uses the Solar Hijri year and season of defense. The English title page must use the corresponding Gregorian calendar year and month.
- **Cover Color:** The color is not arbitrary. It is dictated by the faculty and department:
  - **Humanities (Law, Management, Psychology, etc.):** Crimson Red
  - **Engineering (Civil, Electrical, Computer, Architecture):** Gray
  - **Agriculture & Natural Resources:** Green
  - **Basic Sciences (Geology, Math):** Navy Blue
- **Degree Type on Cover:** Students in Law, Art & Architecture, Humanities & Social Sciences, Theology & Philosophy, Management, and Economics **must** use `M.A.`. Other faculties use `M.Sc.`.
- **Title Page Specifications:** Detailed font sizes for different elements on the cover are provided (e.g., `B Nazanin` 20 Bold for the university name, 16 Bold for the author's name). The file contains a mock-up that must be followed exactly.
- **Submission:** The final version is submitted after a successful defense and the complete approval of requested corrections by the supervisor, advisor, and jury.
