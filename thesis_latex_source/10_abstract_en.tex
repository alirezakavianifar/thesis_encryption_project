\begin{latin}
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{Abstract}}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.5cm}

In the digital era, color images require specialized encryption algorithms due to their large data volume, multi-channel structure, and high adjacent pixel correlation, rendering traditional text-oriented cryptosystems such as AES ineffective. Chaotic systems represent a well-established paradigm for image encryption due to their extreme sensitivity to initial conditions, ergodicity, and pseudo-random properties; however, low-dimensional classical maps such as Logistic and Tent maps suffer from dynamical degradation in finite-precision digital implementations, undermining system security. In this thesis, a five-stage color image encryption algorithm is proposed based on the combination of a continuous three-dimensional Chen chaotic system and nine exponential chaotic maps (ECMs). In the first stage, initial conditions and system control parameters with a precision of 15 decimal digits are configured as the secret key. In the second stage, pixel positions are chaotically permuted across the spatial plane using the sorted continuous state sequences of the Chen attractor. In the third stage, one of the nine exponential chaotic maps is dynamically and independently selected for each individual pixel using an autonomous selector map. In the fourth stage, pixel intensity values are diffused via bitwise XOR operations with the selected chaotic key-bytes. In the fifth stage, an independent secondary diffusion layer is applied to eliminate residual statistical linearities and ensure comprehensive avalanche diffusion across the entire image. The proposed cryptosystem was thoroughly evaluated on standard benchmark color images from the reference USC-SIPI and Kodak databases across multiple resolutions. Experimental findings indicate that the average Shannon entropy of the ciphertext images reaches 7.9973 bits, exhibiting a negligible deviation of only 0.0027 bits from the theoretical optimum of 8 bits. The adjacent pixel correlation coefficient drops dramatically from 0.9989 in plaintext images to 0.0082 in ciphertext images. Moreover, the Number of Pixels Change Rate (NPCR) and Unified Average Changing Intensity (UACI) achieve average values of 96.50\% and 30.93\%, respectively, while complete reversibility (MSE = 0, PSNR = $\infty$) is mathematically and experimentally validated. Key space analysis demonstrates that the total key space exceeds $2^{350}$ ($\approx 10^{105}$), rendering brute-force cryptanalysis computationally infeasible. The principal innovation of this research lies in the pixel-wise dynamic switching among nine exponential chaotic systems, providing elevated structural complexity and robust resistance against differential, statistical, and brute-force attacks.

\vspace{0.8cm}
\textbf{Keywords:} Color Image Encryption; Exponential Chaotic Systems (ECM); Chen Chaotic System; Permutation and Diffusion; Shannon Entropy; Key Space Analysis.
\end{latin}
